### 1. External structural source (verified)

The recursive tuple notation underlying `DoctrineCore^k` traces to:

> Alexander Warren London, MSc., AICP Cand., ENV SP — Lead Design Theorist, Primary Design Co., Sphere-Based Design Researcher. *"High-Level Space Fields: A Recursive Graph Framework for AI-Assisted Spatial Modeling, Computational Geometry, and Multi-Scale Adjacency."* February 2025. Definition 1, Section 2.1.
>
> Source manuscript on file: `HLSFs-Foundation_MANUSCRIPT_0 (oa) refs.docx`.

Definition 1 of that paper defines:

`K_n^k = (T_n^k, V_n^k, E_n^k)`

— a recursive graph tuple at dimension *n*, recursion level *k*, composed of triangles, vertices, and adjacency edges. This is a geometric/spatial modeling construct (urban planning, computational geometry, materials science, routing). **The London paper contains no reference to philosophical weighting, governance fields, or the Core 4.** The only element borrowed is the tuple *notation/shape* — not content, not intent, not application domain.

During a working review of London's paper, Bryan Spruk raised — as an offhand/joke suggestion — inserting the Core 4 governance philosophers (Kant, Locke, Hume, Spinoza) into the London tuple shape. The build partner in that session (GPT) identified the structural fit as viable and the two proceeded to construct it out in that session. This is the actual point of invention for:

`DoctrineCore^k = (Kant^k, Locke^k, Hume^k, Spinoza^k)`

with initial balanced weights `Kant = Locke = Hume = Spinoza = 0.25`, later revised to `0.225` each with `Softmax Advisory = 0.10`.

**This step — the substitution of philosophical fields into the tuple shape — is original to Bryan Spruk and that build session. It is not present in, derived from, or endorsed by London's paper.**
